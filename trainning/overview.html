Here we nurture the students to boost up their technical knowledge, talents and personality to face the industry requirements.

Objectives of Training and Placement Department

  • To maintain close liaison with industries / institutions / organizations to facilitate placements of the students.
  • To arrange for all recruitment drives and other related activities.
  • To plan and organize career talks & personality development programmes.
  • To upgrade the student's skill sets commensurate with the expectations of the industry.
  • To generate awareness amongst the students with respect to future career options available for them.
  • To motivate and counsel students about Industry practices and improving their soft skills.
  • To give Industry feedback to the departments concerned for improving student's skill set.
  • To Assist faculty in inviting speakers for Guest Lectures and Technical Workshops.
