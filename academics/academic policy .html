📝Examination Framework

Each semester includes continuous internal assessment (CIA) and a university-conducted semester-end examination (SEE). CIA components ensure regular evaluation of student progress.

  • Mid-Semester Test – 20 marks
  • Assignment/Quiz/Viva – 10 marks
  • Attendance & Conduct – 10 marks
  • Semester End Exam – 60 marks
  • Practical Examination – as per syllabus

📊Grading System

Academic performance is evaluated on a 10-point CGPA scale as mandated by WBSCT & VE & SD. Grade cards are issued within 30 days of result publication.
