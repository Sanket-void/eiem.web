Research Vision at EIEM

EIEM's Research & Development policy encourages every faculty member and final-year student to engage in meaningful research. The institute provides seed grants, equipment access, and MoU-backed collaborations to translate ideas into impactful innovations.

🏆Faculty Research Highlights

EIEM faculty members regularly publish in Scopus-indexed, SCI, and UGC-CARE listed journals. The institute provides financial support of up to ₹50,000 per paper publication in high-impact journals.

  • 80+ publications in last 3 years
  • 6 patents filed (2 granted)
  • 4 faculty pursuing Ph.D.
  • Conference sponsorship up to ₹25,000
  • Industry-funded research: 3 active projects

🚀Student Innovation Programme

The EIEM Innovation Hub supports student-led projects, hackathons, and start-up ideas from concept to prototype. Annual TechVision festival showcases the best student innovations.

  • Annual seed grant: ₹10,000 per team
  • Prototype lab with 3D printers & CNC tools
  • Mentorship by industry experts
  • TechVision Festival (Annual)
  • Smart India Hackathon participation
