Vision 2030: Engineering Tomorrow's Leaders

EIEM envisions becoming a nationally recognized institution for quality technical education, research-driven pedagogy, and industry-integrated learning by 2030 — anchored in values of integrity, innovation, and inclusivity.

📌 Strategic Objectives
Academic Excellence Research Promotion Industry Collaboration Student Development Faculty Enhancement Infrastructure Upgradation Digital Transformation Community Outreach

📚Academic Development

Curriculum revision in alignment with NEP 2020 guidelines, introduction of interdisciplinary electives, enhanced project-based learning modules, and mandatory industry internships for all undergraduate programmes.

  • Outcome-Based Education (OBE) implementation
  • Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) compliance
  • Annual curriculum review with industry experts
  • E-learning integration with LMS portal

🏗️Infrastructure Planning

Phased development of smart classrooms, advanced computing labs, research incubation centres, and modernisation of existing laboratory infrastructure across all departments.

  • Smart classroom rollout: 12 rooms by 2026
  • High-performance computing cluster
  • Innovation and Start-up Hub
  • Library digitisation and NDLI integration
