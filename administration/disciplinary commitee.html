Disciplinary Committee

Upholding academic integrity, student conduct standards and institutional values with fairness, transparency and due process

The Disciplinary Committee of EIEM is constituted by the Principal to investigate complaints of misconduct, academic dishonesty and breaches of institutional rules. The committee operates on principles of natural justice — every accused student has the right to be heard. All proceedings are confidential. The committee's decisions are subject to appeal to the Governing Body.

Process
Disciplinary Process
Initiation

Complaint Received

A complaint is submitted in writing to the Principal's office or the Disciplinary Committee by any faculty member, student or staff. Anonymous complaints may also be considered if prima facie credible.

Scrutiny

Preliminary Assessment

The committee reviews the complaint within 3 working days and decides whether a formal inquiry is warranted. Frivolous complaints are dismissed at this stage with reasons recorded.

Notice

Notice to Accused

A formal show-cause notice is served to the accused student/staff. They are given a minimum of 5 working days to respond in writing and present their side of the matter.

Inquiry

Formal Inquiry

A committee meeting is convened. Both parties may present evidence, call witnesses and submit documents. All proceedings are recorded and kept confidential.

Decision

Recommendation & Action

The committee passes its recommendation by majority vote. The Principal issues the final order. The decision is communicated to the accused in writing within 2 working days.

Appeal

Right of Appeal

The accused may appeal to the Governing Body within 15 days of receiving the order. The Governing Body's decision is final and binding.

📋 Right of Appeal

Any student or staff member who disagrees with a disciplinary decision has the right to appeal to the Governing Body of EIEM within 15 working days of receiving the written order. The appeal must be submitted in writing with supporting documents to the Registrar's office. A nominal appeal fee of ₹500 (refundable if appeal is upheld) is applicable. The Governing Body shall constitute a review panel within 21 days and deliver its decision. This decision is final and binding on all parties.
