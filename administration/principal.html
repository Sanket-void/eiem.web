About the Principal

Mr. Chandan Chowdhury leads Elitte Institute of Engineering & Management with a vision to cultivate future-ready engineers and innovators. With nearly three decades of academic and research experience, he has shaped the institution into a hub of technical excellence, research, and industry collaboration.
