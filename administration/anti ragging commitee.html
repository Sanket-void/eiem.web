Anti-Ragging Committee

A dedicated body committed to maintaining a ragging-free, safe and respectful campus for all students at EIEM

The Anti-Ragging Committee at EIEM is constituted as per the UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009 and AICTE guidelines. The committee is responsible for investigating complaints, recommending appropriate action and conducting awareness campaigns to ensure every student feels safe and respected on campus.

Committee Mandate
Functions & Responsibilities
Receive and investigate all complaints of ragging received through any channel — oral, written or online
Recommend appropriate punishment after due inquiry to the Principal and Governing Body
Conduct awareness campaigns, orientation programmes and interactive sessions with first-year students
Ensure availability and visibility of anti-ragging notices, helpline numbers and complaint mechanisms across campus
Liaise with police and civil authorities in cases where criminal action is warranted
Submit annual reports to UGC, AICTE and other regulatory bodies as required
Monitor student hostels and common areas through surprise visits during sensitive periods such as fresher orientation week
