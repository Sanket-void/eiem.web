Anti-Ragging Policy

EIEM maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against all forms of ragging. Our campus is a safe, inclusive and respectful environment for every student.

🚨 UGC Anti-Ragging Helpline: 1800-180-5522 | EIEM Helpline: +91 9163316560 | Available 24×7

This policy is framed in compliance with the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India's directives, UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009, and AICTE guidelines. All students, parents and guardians are required to submit the mandatory anti-ragging undertaking at the time of admission. Violation of this policy is a criminal offence under Indian Penal Code Sections 339, 340, 341 and 323.

🚫

Zero Tolerance. No Exceptions.

Elitte Institute of Engineering & Management takes a firm and uncompromising stand against ragging in any form. Every complaint will be investigated thoroughly and strict action — including expulsion and criminal prosecution — will follow.

Definition
What Constitutes Ragging?
🗣️
Verbal Abuse
Any act that involves verbal taunting, using derogatory language, making fun of personal characteristics, or psychologically tormenting a student.
👊
Physical Harm
Any physical contact, assault, forced exercise, sleep deprivation or any bodily harm inflicted on a student by seniors or peers.
💻
Cyberbullying
Harassment through digital platforms, social media, messaging apps or any online medium targeting a student's dignity or mental health.
🎭
Forced Acts
Compelling a student to do any act that they are unwilling to perform, including tasks related to personal services, embarrassing performances or rituals.
🧪
Substance Coercion
Forcing or pressuring students to consume alcohol, drugs or any controlled substance against their will.
💬
Sexual Harassment
Any unwelcome sexual conduct, comment, gesture or demand, whether in person or online, directed at a student constitutes ragging and sexual harassment.
Obligations
Student Obligations
1
All students must submit a signed anti-ragging undertaking (self and parent/guardian) at the time of admission and annually thereafter.
2
Witnessing an act of ragging and not reporting it to authorities makes a student equally culpable and subject to disciplinary action.
3
Students must not incite, abet or participate in ragging in any form — directly or indirectly, inside or outside the campus.
4
Fresher students have the right to refuse any request by seniors that makes them uncomfortable, and to immediately report the same to the Anti-Ragging Committee.
5
False complaints made with malicious intent are also punishable and will be investigated accordingly.
Penalties
Penalties for Ragging
Nature of OffenceSeverityPenalty
Verbal taunting, teasing or mild harassmentMinorWritten warning, community service, counselling
Psychological harassment, group intimidationModerateSuspension for 1 semester, withholding marks/results
Physical assault, coercion, cyberbullyingSevereExpulsion from the institution, FIR with police
Sexual harassment, grave injury or criminal offenceCriticalExpulsion + criminal prosecution under IPC
