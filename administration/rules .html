Rules & Regulations

Guidelines governing academic integrity, conduct and institutional life at Elitte Institute of Engineering & Management

The Rules and Regulations of Elitte Institute of Engineering & Management are framed in accordance with AICTE guidelines, WBSCT & VE & SD norms and the institution's own charter of excellence. All students, faculty and staff are expected to familiarise themselves with these rules and uphold them with integrity. These regulations are reviewed annually and updated as required by regulatory authorities.

Academic
Academic Rules
01
Attendance Requirement
A minimum of 75% attendance in theory and practical classes is mandatory for all students to be eligible to appear in semester examinations. Medical exemptions require certified documentation submitted to the Principal's office within 7 days.
02
Examination Conduct
Students must carry their valid identity card during all examinations. Possession of unauthorised materials, electronic devices or communication with other candidates during examinations will result in immediate disqualification and disciplinary action.
03
Academic Integrity
Plagiarism in assignments, projects or thesis work is strictly prohibited. All submissions are subject to similarity checks. Plagiarism above 25% will attract penalties ranging from assignment cancellation to suspension.
04
Laboratory & Workshop
Students must adhere to safety protocols in all labs and workshops. Protective equipment must be worn at all times. Any damage to equipment due to negligence will be recovered from the student concerned.
Conduct
Code of Conduct
1
All students must maintain respectful and courteous behaviour towards faculty, staff, fellow students and visitors at all times within the campus and at external events representing EIEM.
2
Use of mobile phones is strictly prohibited inside classrooms, examination halls and laboratories. Phones must be kept on silent mode in library and study areas.
3
Students are expected to dress appropriately and professionally. Formal attire is required on institute days, examinations and industry visits. The dress code policy is displayed at all entry points.
4
Smoking, consumption of alcohol or any controlled substance is strictly prohibited on campus premises. Violation is grounds for immediate expulsion.
5
Any form of verbal, physical or cyberbullying, harassment or discrimination based on gender, caste, religion or ethnicity is a punishable offence as per IPC and institutional rules.
6
Students must not engage in political activities, unauthorised assemblies, distribution of pamphlets or any activity that disrupts the academic environment of the campus.
7
Permission from the Head of Department and Principal is required for any student event, external visit, industrial trip or off-campus activity conducted under the institute's name.

⚠️ Important Notice

Violation of these rules may result in disciplinary action including suspension, expulsion or referral to law enforcement authorities as appropriate. Students are encouraged to report violations to the Disciplinary Committee or through the official Grievance Portal. All complaints will be treated with strict confidentiality.

Library & IT
Library & IT Usage Rules
A
Library Usage
Books must be returned within the stipulated loan period. A fine of ₹5 per day per book is charged for overdue returns. Damage or loss of library property will be charged at replacement cost plus a penalty.
B
Internet & Network Policy
The institute's internet and network infrastructure is for academic purposes only. Access to social media, streaming platforms and non-academic content is restricted. Misuse of network resources is subject to disciplinary action.
C
Software & Data
Installation of unlicensed software on institute computers is prohibited. Students are personally responsible for data stored on institute systems. The institute reserves the right to audit system usage logs.
