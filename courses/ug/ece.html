About the Programme

What is B.Tech in ECE?

A 4-year comprehensive undergraduate programme that integrates electronics, communication systems, signal processing, and emerging technologies like IoT and VLSI.

📡

Programme Overview

The B.Tech ECE at EIEM provides strong foundation in analog & digital electronics, communication systems, embedded programming, VLSI design, and hands-on training with modern tools like MATLAB, Cadence, Arduino, and Raspberry Pi.

📋

Affiliation & Approval

Approved by AICTE, affiliated to Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), West Bengal. Industry-oriented curriculum with focus on cutting-edge technologies.
