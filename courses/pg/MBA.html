About the Programme

What is the Master of Business Administration?

A prestigious 2-year postgraduate programme designed to develop future business leaders, managers, and entrepreneurs with practical industry exposure.

💼

Programme Overview

The MBA at EIEM combines strong theoretical foundations with real-world case studies, live projects, industry internships, and personality development. Students can specialize in Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, Operations, or Business Analytics.

📋

Affiliation & Approval

Approved by AICTE, affiliated to Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), West Bengal. Curriculum is regularly updated with inputs from industry experts and corporate leaders.
