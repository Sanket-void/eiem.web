About the Programme

What is the Diploma in CST?

A structured, hands-on technical programme designed for Class 10 pass students who want to enter the IT industry faster with practical skills.

🎯

Programme Overview

The 3-year Diploma in Computer Science & Technology (CST) is a WBSCTVESD-approved programme offered by EIEM. It is structured into 6 semesters, combining theoretical foundations with practical lab sessions, live projects, and industry internships. Students graduate with real-world skills in programming, networking, database management, and modern web technologies — making them job-ready from Day 1.

📋

Affiliation & Approval

Approved by AICTE, affiliated to West Bengal State Council of Technical & Vocational Education and Skill Development (WBSCTVESD). The programme follows a curriculum benchmarked against industry requirements and is regularly updated in collaboration with our 200+ industry partners including TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant and HCL.

Admission

Eligibility & Admission Process

Simple, transparent, and merit-based. Here's what you need to apply.

01

Educational Qualification

Passed Class 10 (Madhyamik or equivalent) with Mathematics and Science. Minimum 35% aggregate marks required. Science background preferred but not mandatory.

02

Age Limit

Minimum age: 14 years. No upper age limit for regular students. SC/ST candidates get additional relaxation as per WBSCTVESD norms.

03

Documents Required

Class 10 Marksheet & Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Passport-size Photos (4), Caste Certificate (if applicable), Migration Certificate (if from other board).
