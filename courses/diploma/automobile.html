About the Programme

What is the Diploma in Automobile Engineering?

A hands-on technical programme designed for Class 10 pass students who want to build a successful career in the booming automotive and EV industry.

🚗

Programme Overview

The 3-year Diploma in Automobile Engineering is a WBSCTVESD-approved programme offered by EIEM. It combines strong theoretical knowledge with extensive workshop practice, live projects, vehicle maintenance training, and industry internships. Students gain expertise in engines, transmission, electrical systems, vehicle diagnostics, and emerging EV technology.

📋

Affiliation & Approval

Approved by AICTE, affiliated to West Bengal State Council of Technical & Vocational Education and Skill Development (WBSCTVESD). Curriculum is regularly updated with inputs from leading automotive companies like Tata Motors, Maruti Suzuki, Mahindra, Bosch, and Hero MotoCorp.
