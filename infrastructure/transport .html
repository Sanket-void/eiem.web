About Transport Facility

EIEM operates a robust fleet of air-conditioned and non-AC buses covering over 30 routes across Kolkata, Howrah, North 24-Parganas, and surrounding districts. The transport department, staffed by trained drivers and conductors, ensures punctuality, safety, and comfort. All vehicles are CCTV-monitored and GPS-tracked in real time, with dedicated routes for ladies.

Safety & Technology

Fleet Safety Features

📍

GPS Tracking

All buses are equipped with real-time GPS tracking accessible by the transport control room and parents.

📹

CCTV Cameras

High-definition CCTV cameras installed inside every bus with 24-hour footage storage.

🚨

Emergency SOS

Panic button installed in each bus for immediate assistance from campus security and local authorities.

🔧

Regular Maintenance

Fleet undergoes monthly technical inspections and servicing to ensure zero breakdowns and passenger safety.

👨‍✈️

Trained Drivers

All drivers hold valid commercial licenses and undergo annual safety and defensive driving training programmes.

🛡

Insurance Coverage

All vehicles and passengers are covered under comprehensive insurance for any unforeseen incidents.
