Key Features

What Our Network Offers

Designed to support modern academic workflows, from cloud computing labs to digital research and online examinations.

01

NME-ICT Compliant Network

Meets all National Mission on Education through ICT guidelines, ensuring eligibility for NPTEL, SWAYAM, and other national e-learning platforms.

02

LDAP-Based User Authentication

Secure single sign-on (SSO) for all students and staff using centralized LDAP directory, with unique credentials and personalised bandwidth allocation.

03

VPN Access for Remote Learning

Students and faculty can securely access campus library databases, internal portals, and lab resources remotely via institute-provided VPN credentials.

04

e-Journal & Database Subscriptions

Campus-wide IP-based access to IEEE Xplore Digital Library, Springer, Elsevier ScienceDirect, J-Gate, NDLI, and Web of Science with no per-article charges.

05

Network Monitoring & Analytics

24×7 real-time network monitoring dashboard with automated alerts, bandwidth utilisation reports, and predictive fault detection to maximise uptime.

06

Smart Bandwidth Management

Quality-of-Service (QoS) policies prioritise academic and research traffic. Streaming and non-academic traffic is throttled during peak examination hours automatically.

Usage Policy

Acceptable Use Guidelines

All users of the campus internet facility are expected to adhere to the institutional Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for responsible and ethical use.

✅ Permitted Activities

  • Accessing academic databases, research papers, and e-journals
  • Using NPTEL, SWAYAM, Coursera, and other MOOC platforms
  • Downloading course materials, lecture notes, and software tools
  • Online examinations, virtual labs, and simulation software
  • Video conferencing for classes, seminars, and webinars
  • Professional networking on LinkedIn and GitHub
  • Accessing cloud-based development environments and IDE tools

🚫 Prohibited Activities

  • Accessing obscene, offensive, or unlawful content
  • Peer-to-peer (P2P) file sharing of copyrighted material
  • Attempting to bypass firewall, proxy, or content filters
  • Installing unauthorised software or network scanning tools
  • Using torrents or excessive bandwidth for personal streaming
  • Hacking, phishing, or any malicious network activity
  • Sharing personal credentials or misusing institute login accounts
