In Action

Practices Across Domains

Concrete initiatives and policies that distinguish EIEM as a model institution in technical education.

📘 Academic & Learning

01

Outcome-Based Education (OBE)

All programmes follow OBE guidelines aligned with NBA/NAAC criteria, with clearly defined Course Outcomes (COs) mapped to Programme Outcomes (POs).

02

Continuous Internal Assessment

Regular class tests, assignments, and surprise quizzes ensure consistent evaluation and timely identification of students needing academic support.

03

Mentor-Mentee Programme

Every student is assigned a faculty mentor who tracks academic progress, counsels on career goals, and provides personalised guidance throughout the programme.

04

NPTEL & MOOC Integration

Students are encouraged and given credit for completing certified online courses on SWAYAM, Coursera, and edX platforms as supplementary academic credits.

🏭 Industry & Innovation

05

Expert Guest Lecture Series

Industry experts, alumni in leadership roles, and researchers are invited regularly to share real-world insights and emerging technology trends with students.

06

Industrial Visits & Exposure

Mandatory industrial visits to manufacturing plants, IT companies, and research labs give students hands-on understanding of professional environments and processes.

07

Start-Up Incubation Support

The EIEM IIC cell provides seed funding assistance, mentoring, coworking space, and networking opportunities to student entrepreneurs with viable business ideas.

08

Patent Filing Assistance

Faculty and students are supported with legal guidance, documentation help, and filing fees for patents arising from in-house research and innovation projects.

Quality Process

Annual Quality Enhancement Cycle

A structured annual cycle drives continuous improvement in academic quality, infrastructure, and student outcomes.

Step 01 — June

Curriculum Review & Revision

Board of Studies meetings are convened with industry experts and faculty to review syllabi, incorporate emerging technologies, and align outcomes with current job market demands.

Step 02 — August

Faculty Development Programme (FDP)

Before each academic year, all faculty undergo mandatory FDPs on pedagogical innovation, research methodology, digital tools, and accreditation processes.

Step 03 — Ongoing

Student Feedback & Attainment Monitoring

Mid-semester and end-semester student feedback surveys and CO/PO attainment calculations are reviewed by the IQAC to identify areas for pedagogical improvement.

Step 04 — January

Annual IQAC Review Meeting

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell compiles the Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR), reviews accreditation metrics, and sets improvement targets for the next cycle.

Step 05 — April

Placement Outcome Analysis & Gap Bridging

Post-placement season, a detailed analysis of skill gaps is conducted and findings are fed back into the curriculum and co-curricular planning for the next academic year.
