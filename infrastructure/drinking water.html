About the Facility

The EIEM Central Drinking Water System provides 100% purified drinking water across the entire campus through state-of-the-art Reverse Osmosis (RO) plants combined with UV disinfection and mineral cartridges. The facility ensures every student, faculty member, and staff has access to safe, great-tasting water at multiple convenient locations. Water quality is tested regularly by NABL-accredited laboratories.

Facilities & Access

Campus Water Network

💧

Central RO Plant

8,000 LPH state-of-the-art RO system with pre-treatment, high-pressure pumps, and automated backwash.

🧊

Chilled Water Coolers

Energy-efficient water coolers with instant cooling at all academic blocks, library, cafeteria & hostels.

📱

Smart Monitoring

IoT-based real-time TDS & flow monitoring with alerts for any deviation in water quality.

🚰

Hostel & Mess Supply

Dedicated RO lines with storage tanks ensuring uninterrupted supply in all hostels and mess areas.

🔬

Water Testing Lab

On-campus facility for rapid testing of pH, TDS, turbidity, and bacteriological parameters.

♻️

Waste Water Recycling

RO reject water is reused for gardening and flushing, promoting sustainability.

Water Usage Guidelines

All students are requested to use water responsibly. Report any leakage immediately to the maintenance team. Do not tamper with RO units or coolers. Clean drinking water is a shared resource — let’s keep it safe and pure for everyone.
