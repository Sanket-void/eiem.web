Workshop Sections

Hands-On Craftsmanship & Engineering

The EIEM Central Workshop is divided into six specialized sections, each equipped with professional-grade machinery and supervised by experienced technical staff.

🔩
Fitting & Smithy Shop
Students learn precision fitting, filing, chiselling, and metal joining techniques. The smithy section includes coal and gas forges, anvils, hammers, and tongs for metal forming and forging operations — fundamental skills for all mechanical engineers.
Bench VicesForgesAnvilsPrecision Tools
🔄
Machining & Lathe Shop
Equipped with centre lathes, milling machines, drilling machines, and shaper machines. Students perform turning, boring, threading, and surface finishing operations under expert supervision, developing core machining competencies.
Centre LathesMilling MachinesDrill PressesGrinders
🔥
Welding & Sheet Metal Shop
Modern arc welding, MIG/MAG, TIG, and gas welding stations alongside sheet metal cutting, bending, and forming equipment. Emphasizes joint quality, safety protocols, and metallurgical understanding essential for structural fabrication.
TIG WeldingMIG/MAGSheet BendersGuillotines
🪵
Carpentry & Pattern Making Shop
Traditional and modern woodworking tools including band saws, jointers, planers, and lathes for creating patterns, moulds, and structural elements. Develops spatial understanding and precision craftsmanship.
Band SawsWood LathesJointersRouters
🖨️
CNC & Rapid Prototyping Shop
Houses CNC turning centres, CNC milling machines, and FDM 3D printers. Students learn G-code programming, CAM software, and additive manufacturing techniques to produce precision components and functional prototypes.
CNC LatheCNC Mill3D PrintersCAM Software
⚡
Electrical Wiring & Plumbing Shop
Practical training in domestic wiring, switchboard assembly, pipeline fitting, and plumbing tasks. Teaches students about service infrastructure, circuit layouts, and maintenance skills applicable in civil and electrical engineering.
Wiring PanelsPipe FittingsTest EquipmentSafety Gear

Where Ideas Become Real

The EIEM Workshop is more than just a practical training space — it's a maker's environment where engineering concepts take physical form. With dedicated safety training, PPE for all students, and experienced technicians guiding every session, students graduate with the confidence to build, repair, and innovate.
