About the Cafeteria

The EIEM Cafeteria is a spacious, air-conditioned dining facility designed to serve the nutritional needs of over 3,000 students, faculty, and staff daily. Operated under strict FSSAI guidelines, the cafeteria offers a wide variety of freshly prepared meals, snacks, and beverages at subsidised rates. With a focus on hygiene, taste, and affordability, the cafeteria is a hub of community and culture on campus.

Rules & Policies

Cafeteria Guidelines

🧼

Hygiene First

All kitchen staff undergo regular health checks. FSSAI standards are strictly followed at all times.

🚯

No Littering

Students must dispose of waste in designated bins. Maintaining cleanliness is a shared responsibility.

🔇

Noise Control

Please maintain a moderate noise level to ensure a comfortable dining environment for everyone.

⏱

Queue Discipline

Orderly queuing at all counters is mandatory. Students are expected to respect each other's space.

📵

No Outside Food

Outside food is discouraged inside the cafeteria premises to maintain hygiene standards.

♻️

Eco-Friendly

Single-use plastics are banned. Eco-friendly packaging and biodegradable cutlery are used throughout.

📢

Special Dietary Requirements

Students with specific dietary needs (diabetic, gluten-free, vegan) may submit a request to the Student Affairs Office. The cafeteria management will arrange tailored meals wherever feasible.
