🎓 Polytechnic Diploma Programmes
1️⃣

1st Year Admission Eligibility

Direct entry into the first semester of the Diploma Engineering programme

10th

Madhyamik or Equivalent Board Examination

Candidates must have passed the Madhyamik (Class 10) or an equivalent recognized board examination with the following compulsory subjects:

  • Science / Physical Science
  • Mathematics
2️⃣

2nd Year Lateral Entry Eligibility

Direct entry into the third semester of the Diploma Engineering programme

10+2

Higher Secondary or Equivalent Examination

Candidates who have passed the Higher Secondary (10+2) or equivalent examination with any of the following subjects:

  • Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry
  • Biology, Agriculture, Computer Science
  • Electronics, Information Technology, Biotechnology
  • Technical Vocational Subject, Engineering Graphics
  • Business Studies, Entrepreneurship
OR
ITI

ITI (Industrial Training Institute) Certificate

Candidates who have successfully completed a 2-year ITI course approved by NCVT (National Council for Vocational Training) after passing Class 10 or its equivalent examination.

📌 Important Notes & Reservations
  • There is no upper age limit for the admission of Diploma in Engineering & Technology at EIEM.
  • There will be a reservation of 20% seats for female candidates uniformly in all categories as per the directives of WBSCTVE & SD.
  • There will be 10% reservation for the Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) as per the directives of WBSCTVE & SD.
  • All reservations and admissions are subject to the guidelines and norms laid down by WBSCT & VE & SD, Government of West Bengal.
  • Admission is subject to merit, document verification, and seat availability in the selected programme.
